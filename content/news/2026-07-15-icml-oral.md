Priya Nair presented [*Meta-learning protein fitness landscapes from few measurements*](/publications#nair2026metaprot) in the oral session on learning with limited data.

The paper shows that a model meta-trained on 40 deep mutational scanning assays can predict fitness on a new protein from 20 labeled variants, where standard fine-tuning needs several hundred.
