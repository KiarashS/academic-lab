## Computing

The lab has a GPU cluster with 32 NVIDIA H100 GPUs, shared with two other groups in the department. Members get an account in their first week; ask the lab manager.

## Space

Students and postdocs have desks in Room 404 of Example Hall. The meeting room next door can be booked through the department calendar.

## Partner labs

Wet-lab experiments run in the [Protein Design Group](/collaborators)'s facilities at the Example Institute of Technology.
